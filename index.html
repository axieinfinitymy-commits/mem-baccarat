How it works

Browser simulation · no payments · no withdrawals
01

Become House

A demo account receives RM50,000 in fake credits and controls a table.

02

Players bet

Players choose Player, Banker or Tie and commit simulated credits.

03

Deal

The embedded Baccarat engine applies standard third-card rules.

04

Settle

Each fake-credit wager is resolved against the House.

05

Economics

Configurable platform fee and operator share are calculated from House gross wins.

06

Audit

Every round stores cards, bets, result, settlement, fees and timestamp in demo state.
